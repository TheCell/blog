After finishing Keeper from Double Fine my thoughts were: That was a refreshingly different game compared to other games for the last couple of years.

I love taking pictures in games and this one is just on another level. The lighting, the composition, the camera settings are excellent and the game itself is just wonderful. The landscape looks like it's drawn and has lots and lots of details. The world morphs, bends and stretches. It is a giant fever dream.

It's the perfect game if you like new experiences and experimental stuff. There is a story in it but don't expect rich gameplay.
